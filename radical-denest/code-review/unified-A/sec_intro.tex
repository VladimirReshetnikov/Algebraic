\section{Scope, material, and method}
\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{The object under analysis}
The file \code{src/original/Strad.wl} (585 physical lines, SHA-256
\path{693a2825...cccc93}, byte-identical to the copies shipped with the three
reviews) defines a radical denester for the Wolfram Language. Its entry point
\code{Strad[expr]} suppresses diagnostics and calls \code{DenestRadicals3}, which
factors the input, marks every subexpression of the form
$\text{radicand}^{p/q}$ whose radicand is an explicit algebraic number, groups
some products of markers, and hands each marked value to the search engine
\code{denest11}. The engine chooses a root order $q$, forms $\rho=\beta^q$, and
searches over multipliers $m$ for a lower-degree factor of $X^q-m\rho$ over an
algebraic coefficient field. Helpers implement a minimal-polynomial based
denominator rationalizer, nesting-depth annotations, and a custom factorizer.
Appendix~\ref{app:original} reproduces the file with line numbers; all
``\src{$n$}'' references point there.

\subsection{The three reviews}
Three independent reviews were written on the same day, 5~September 2026, and
are kept under \code{reports/review-1}, \code{review-2} and \code{review-3}. We
refer to them as \RI, \RII\ and \RIII. Table~\ref{tab:reviewsummary}
summarizes them. They agree on the architecture, on the most serious defect
(the principal-root reset), and on the broad repair strategy. They differ in the
defects they add, in their emphasis, and in a few predictions that the live
experiments below refine or overturn. Crucially, \emph{none of the three ran
the program}: each states explicitly that its Wolfram tests were not executed
and that its proposed replacement component is unexecuted.

\subsection{What this document adds}
\begin{enumerate}
\item A single reconstruction of how the code works, with actual search traces
      (Section~\ref{sec:code}), and proofs of the statements that justify or
      refute its steps (Section~\ref{sec:math}).
\item A finding-by-finding comparison of the three reviews, including the
      places where a review over- or under-stated a defect
      (Section~\ref{sec:reviews}).
\item Executed experiments: 90 hand-chosen probes of the original program, 20
      helper-level probes, 340 randomized inputs in ten structured families, the
      reviews' own regression suites, and the same batteries against the
      corrected version (Section~\ref{sec:experiments}).
\item A unified, numbered catalogue of 27 defects and shortcomings, each with
      its status (confirmed, refined, refuted, or theoretical), its source
      lines, and the reviews that reported it (Section~\ref{sec:findings}).
\item A corrected implementation, \code{src/corrected/StradFixed.wl}, that
      keeps the original architecture and search heuristics but makes
      correctness independent of them (Section~\ref{sec:corrected},
      Appendix~\ref{app:fixed}).
\item A prioritized list of future improvements (Section~\ref{sec:future}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Conventions}
Throughout, $\beta$ denotes the exact algebraic number handed to the search
engine (the ``problem''), $q\ge2$ the chosen root order, $\rho=\beta^q$ the
``radicand'', $m$ a multiplier, and $P_m$ the minimal polynomial over $\Q$ of
the principal value $(m\rho)^{1/q}$. Rational powers of complex numbers have
their Wolfram Language meaning: $z^{p/q}=\exp\!\big(\tfrac pq\log z\big)$ with the
principal logarithm, so $\Arg z^{1/q}\in(-\pi/q,\pi/q]$. The syntactic radical
depth $\rdepth(E)$ is the maximal number of \code{Power[_, _Rational]} nodes on
a root-to-leaf path of the expression tree of $E$; it is a property of the
representation, not of the number.

Experiment identifiers such as A02 or D06 refer to the rows of the tables in
Section~\ref{sec:experiments}. Every result quoted in the text is copied from
those mechanically generated tables or from the recorded logs.
