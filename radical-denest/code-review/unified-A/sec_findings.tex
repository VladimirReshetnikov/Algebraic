\section{Unified catalogue of defects and shortcomings}
\label{sec:findings}
Each entry gives the severity, the source lines, the reviews that report it,
the experimental evidence, and the repair adopted in \code{StradFixed.wl}
(Section~\ref{sec:corrected}). Severity: \textbf{critical} = wrong value can
be returned; \textbf{high} = crash, garbage output, or uncontrolled resource
use; \textbf{medium} = wrong or misleading behaviour without a wrong value;
\textbf{low} = maintenance.

\newcommand{\finding}[6]{\subsubsection*{#1\quad #2}\label{u:#3}\noindent\textit{Severity:} #4.\quad\textit{Source:} #5.\quad\textit{Reviews:} #6.\par}

\subsection{Wrong values}

\finding{U1}{The candidate filter certifies against $(\beta^q)^{1/q}$ instead of $\beta$}{1}{critical}{\src{94, 125--127}}{\RI\ F01, \RII\ B1, \RIII\ F1}
\emph{Evidence.} D01, D04, D05 (engine), C02, C03 (wrapper); fuzz F5 39/40
and F6 17/40 wrong. The wrapper's grouping rules and its acceptance of
exponents $p/q$ with $p>1$ deliver exactly the values outside the principal
sector of Proposition~\ref{prop:principal}. The reviews' hedge that a leading
minus sign is kept outside the marker is confirmed (C01 is right).
\emph{Repair.} \code{tryMultiplier} receives \code{problem} and selects with
\code{CertifiedEqualQ[#, problem]}; the orbit of Proposition~\ref{prop:orbit}
is unchanged.

\finding{U2}{The custom factorizer applies power identities without branch conditions}{2}{critical}{\src{527--531, 571--583}}{\RI\ F02/F03, \RII\ B2, \RIII\ F2}
\emph{Evidence.} Symbolic: E01 $\sqrt{z^2}\to z$, E03 $\sqrt{-x-y}\to i\sqrt{x+y}$,
H18 $\sqrt{x^4}\to x^2$, H19 $(x^2)^{1/3}\to x^{2/3}$. Numeric, end to end:
J01/J02 $\sqrt{-(\sqrt2+(-1)^{1/3})}\to i\sqrt{(-1)^{1/3}+\sqrt2}$ (the negative of
the input), J06 the cube-root analogue. \RI's symbolic probe
\code{Sqrt[t + t^2]} does \emph{not} fail (H02, and the passing test in its
own suite). \emph{Repair.} Proposition~\ref{prop:power}: the exponent merge is
applied only when the outer exponent is an integer or the inner exponent is
real in $(-1,1]$; common factors are pulled out of a sum under a fractional
power only when the power is an integer, the extracted factor is a positive
real, or the remaining sum is a positive real. Factoring is applied only to
exact numeric subexpressions and its output is certified against its input.

\subsection{Unsafe acceptance and validation}

\finding{U3}{\code{PossibleZeroQ} with default method is the only certificate}{3}{high}{\src{125--127}}{\RI\ F04, \RII\ \S8.3, \RIII\ F6}
\emph{Evidence.} In 20 of 31 classical rows and 272 of 340 random inputs
the kernel reports \code{PossibleZeroQ::ztest1}: it could not decide and
assumed zero. Because the candidates are exact algebraic numbers and the
correct one is present in the orbit, this ``assumption'' happened to be right
in every observed case, and S04--S06 show that the default method does reject
close non-zero differences. The defect is a missing guarantee, not an
observed false positive. \emph{Repair.} \code{CertifiedEqualQ}: 30-digit
numeric rejection, then \code{RootReduce[a - b] === 0}, then
\code{PossibleZeroQ[..., Method -> "ExactAlgebraics"]}, each under a time limit;
undecided means rejected. No message is emitted.

\finding{U4}{Zero, inexact or non-algebraic multipliers and empty candidate lists are not handled}{4}{high}{\src{111--134, 214--215}}{\RI\ F05, \RII\ B5/B10, \RIII\ F5}
\emph{Evidence.} D02 returns \code{{}[[1]]} after printing ``success'';
D10 returns \code{Outer[][[1]]}; both leave \code{Power::infy} and
\code{Part::partw} messages. \emph{Repair.} Multipliers must be exact,
algebraic and nonzero; polynomial results are type-checked; an empty
certified list is an ordinary ``no candidate'' outcome.

\finding{U15}{The input domain is not enforced}{15}{high}{\src{5, 79, 454}}{\RI\ F10, \RII\ B10, \RIII\ F9}
\emph{Evidence.} K14: a list of two radicals becomes
$(3+2\sqrt2)^{(\sqrt2+\sqrt3)/2}$ because the nesting helpers and the factorizer
encode structure as lists. K12: an inexact input is returned as a machine
number. B07: a \code{Root} object becomes a nested radical. E08: a user
function around a radical triggers seven messages. \emph{Repair.} Lists are
mapped over; inexact input is returned unchanged; \code{Root} input is
converted with \code{ToRadicals} first and then denested; only exact numeric
algebraic subexpressions are factored and marked.

\subsection{Search control}

\finding{U5}{The prime comparator compares a pair with itself}{5}{high}{\src{181}}{\RI\ F06, \RII\ B8, \RIII\ F3}
\emph{Evidence.} S01: \code{Sort} with the predicate \code{#1[[1]] <= #[[2]] &}
returns the list \emph{reversed} (the predicate is \code{False} for every pair
of plain primes and the merge sort then reverses). Consequences (N01): the
ratio-pruning loop of \src{184--189} never runs, and the prefix of
\src{190--194} keeps the largest primes. \emph{Repair.} \code{SortBy[..., First]}.

\finding{U6}{The prefix length uses the pre-deletion length}{6}{high (latent)}{\src{190--194}}{\RIII\ F4}
\emph{Evidence.} S02 and N02: with the comparator repaired, the fixture
$\{2,3,5,7,11,2003\}$ triggers \code{Part::take}. In the original program the
zeroing loop never runs (\U{5}), so the defect is unreachable; it becomes live
as soon as \U{5} is fixed in isolation. \emph{Repair.} Delete first, then take
\code{Min[count, Length]}.

\finding{U7}{The multiplier ``cap'' is not a cap; \code{Ceiling[Log]} misfires}{7}{high}{\src{162--199}}{\RI\ F07, \RII\ B6, \RIII\ F7}
\emph{Evidence.} Proposition~\ref{prop:count}; N05/N06 materialize 1024 and
100\,000 divisors; the largest batch observed in practice was 63 (L04). For
$q=10$ the exact quotient \code{Log[1000]/Log[10]} is not decided by
\code{Ceiling} (R01--R04, L14), so \code{Max[5, ...]} silently falls back to 5.
\emph{Repair.} $t=\max\{t: q^t-1\le\text{cap}\}$ by integer arithmetic; the
post-first-pass product batch is truncated to the cap; a trial budget and a
time budget bound the whole search.

\finding{U12}{The equal-degree gate skips a linear GCD}{12}{medium}{\src{115}}{\RII\ H1}
\emph{Evidence.} D06 versus D07. \emph{Repair.} The GCD is attempted when
$d_m\le d_{\text{best}}$.

\finding{U13}{\code{List @@ Expand[...]} assumes a sum}{13}{low}{\src{224--229}}{\RI\ \S5.1, \RII\ H4, \RIII\ \S6.1}
\emph{Evidence.} Not reachable with exact numeric radicands: \code{Expand} of a
power of a nested radical is a \code{Plus}, and monomial radicands are split by
the kernel's automatic evaluation before they reach the engine
(\code{Sqrt[3 Sqrt[2]]} evaluates to \code{2^(1/4) Sqrt[3]}). \emph{Repair.} An
explicit \code{summands} helper.

\finding{U14}{Composite cofactors of the discriminant are dropped}{14}{low}{\src{173--181}}{\RI\ \S4.4, \RII\ H2, \RIII\ \S6.3}
\emph{Evidence.} Never triggered in the experiments (no ``composite factor''
diagnostic was printed). \emph{Repair.} Kept, but logged.

\finding{U16}{No global budget and no visited set}{16}{medium}{\src{251--432}}{\RI\ F12, \RII\ B7, \RIII\ \S9.3}
\emph{Evidence.} Duplicate multipliers across batches are visible in the traces
(D08 tries 2 and $2\sqrt{3+2\sqrt2}$, then 17, 34, $17\sqrt{\cdot}$, $34\sqrt{\cdot}$);
no runaway was observed on the tested inputs. \emph{Repair.} \code{"MaxTrials"},
\code{"TimeBudget"}, and a visited association keyed by the multiplier.

\finding{U22}{Single-level mode cannot exploit inner denestings}{22}{medium}{\src{7--10, 58--61}}{all, in passing}
\emph{Evidence.} A29 (single level) leaves $\sqrt{3+2\sqrt{3+2\sqrt2}}$ alone
after 12 trials; A28 (all levels) finds $\sqrt{5+2\sqrt2}$. \emph{Repair.} The
default remains single level for compatibility, but a partial solution of
depth $\ge2$ is passed through one further all-level pass.

\finding{U23}{Candidates contain denestable inner surds}{23}{medium}{\src{121--134}}{--}
\emph{Evidence.} Q01--Q08: the roots of the quadratic GCD contain
$\sqrt{9-6\sqrt2}=\sqrt6-\sqrt3$ and \code{Simplify} does not remove it, so 17
of 30 negative-base cube roots keep their depth. \emph{Repair.} Candidate
polishing applies Proposition~\ref{prop:surd} to inner square roots, then
\code{Simplify}; all 30 are denested.

\finding{U24}{``Forced'' multipliers are only initial multipliers}{24}{low}{\src{214--215, 338--378}}{all}
\emph{Evidence.} D12: with $\{1,\sqrt5\}$ the degree-8 trial counts as progress
and a discriminant batch finds $2$. \emph{Repair.} Documented as the
\code{"Multipliers"} option with the same semantics.

\finding{U25}{The search is heuristic and incomplete}{25}{medium}{\src{159--249}}{\RI\ F11, \RII\ H3, \RIII\ F11}
\emph{Evidence.} No false ``denesting exists'' claims were observed; A13 and
the F3 controls are correctly unchanged. No completeness certificate is
possible for this design. \emph{Repair.} None; see Section~\ref{sec:future}.

\subsection{Output quality}

\finding{U9}{``Success'' is not measured against an objective}{9}{medium}{\src{120--134, 309--324, 435--438}}{\RI\ F09, \RII\ H5, \RIII\ F8}
\emph{Evidence.} D07 returns $(1+\sqrt2+\sqrt3)/\sqrt{2(3-\sqrt2+\sqrt3-\sqrt6)}$
for $\sqrt{5+2\sqrt6}$; A30/A31 and L04 return correct but huge quotients;
B01 rewrites $\sqrt{2+\sqrt2}$ as $2^{1/4}\sqrt{1+\sqrt2}$. \emph{Repair.} The
cost $\cost(E)=(\#\code{Root},\ \rdepth,\ \#\text{radical nodes},\ \#\text{atoms},\ \code{LeafCount})$
is compared lexicographically; a result is returned only if it is strictly
cheaper than the input. Candidates are polished by \code{Simplify},
rationalization of the denominator (which turns the L04 quotient into
$\sqrt2+\sqrt3+\sqrt5+\sqrt7$), and inner-surd denesting.

\finding{U20}{The final \code{Simplify} re-nests \code{Root} objects and rewrites unchanged input}{20}{medium}{\src{59--61, 73}}{\RIII\ in passing}
\emph{Evidence.} B07, B01, and the 8 ``equal, rewritten'' F3 outputs.
\emph{Repair.} \code{Simplify} is one of several candidate variants subject to
the cost gate, and \code{Root} input is converted \emph{before} denesting.

\finding{U27}{Quotient candidates are not rationalized}{27}{low}{\src{121--134}}{--}
\emph{Evidence.} L04; the program's own \code{RationalizeDenominator} followed
by \code{Simplify} produces the intended answer (S01 in
Table~\ref{tab:r3QRS}). \emph{Repair.} Part of candidate polishing.

\subsection{Interfaces and session hygiene}

\finding{U8}{Loading the file unprotects \code{Print}}{8}{medium}{\src{2}}{\RI\ F08, \RII\ B9, \RIII\ F10}
\emph{Evidence.} \code{Attributes[Print]} is \code{{Protected}} before and
\code{{}} after \code{Get}. \emph{Repair.} Verbose logging through an option.

\finding{U10}{The marker \code{f} collides with user symbols}{10}{high}{\src{6, 12, 55}}{\RII\ B3}
\emph{Evidence.} M01/E07 (\code{f[1]} becomes \code{1}), E08. \emph{Repair.} A
private-context marker symbol. E09 shows that even the private marker is
handled gracefully.

\finding{U11}{\code{RationalizeDenominator} substitutes \code{x -> 0} into the numerator}{11}{high}{\src{445--450}}{\RII\ B4}
\emph{Evidence.} R05 returns 0 for \code{x/(1 + Sqrt[2])}. \emph{Repair.}
\code{Module}-local variable; non-algebraic denominators are returned
unchanged (J17).

\finding{U19}{\code{Strad} suppresses \code{Print} but not messages}{19}{medium}{\src{3}}{--}
\emph{Evidence.} Ordinary successful calls show \code{N::meprec} and
\code{PossibleZeroQ::ztest1}; invalid input shows \code{Power::infy},
\code{Part::partw}, and others. \emph{Repair.} No message-producing operation
remains on the normal path; failures are caught and turned into ``no
candidate''.

\finding{U17}{Fragile positional state; \code{Throw[, "solution"]}}{17}{low}{\src{253--267, 292, 320}}{\RI\ F13, \RII\ M1, \RIII\ \S9.5}
\emph{Evidence.} Loading emits \code{Syntax::com} for \src{320}. \emph{Repair.}
\code{Throw[Null, "solution"]}; otherwise the record layout is kept for
fidelity, with type checks where records are consumed.

\finding{U18}{Dead settings and unused variables}{18}{low}{\src{95--103, 163, 400}}{\RI\ F14, \RII\ M1, \RIII\ F12}
\code{extrapower}, \code{maxprimes}, \code{best} (in the extra block),
\code{expected}, \code{done}. \emph{Repair.} Removed.

\finding{U21}{\code{combine} does not clean a trailing unit entry}{21}{low}{\src{558--568}}{\RIII\ \S9.5}
\emph{Repair.} Loop bound \code{j <= Length}.

\finding{U26}{All-level mode marks every radical, including $\sqrt{29}$}{26}{low}{\src{9, 11}}{--}
\emph{Evidence.} The A30 trace shows \code{f[Sqrt[29]]} as a ``problem''.
Harmless: the engine returns it at its gate.

\subsection{What is \emph{not} a defect}
The sign-flip loop with its accumulator (\src{30--37}) preserves the product;
one-argument \code{MinimalPolynomial} returning a pure function is valid;
non-strict comparators are permitted by \code{Sort}; \code{Extension ->
Automatic} handles nested radicands (D08); \RIII's level-0 \code{MemberQ}
remark does not apply to exact numeric input; and the rationalizer's formula is
correct including the non-monic case (R01--R10). The engine's search order,
though heuristic, found every classical denesting we tried, usually with the
first or second discriminant batch.
